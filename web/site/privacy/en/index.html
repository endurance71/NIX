Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-12 · effective 12 October 2026

1. General provisions

1. This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) sets out how personal data of users of the NiX mobile application (the “App”) and of the website nix.shipapp.pl (the “Website”) is processed, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (the “GDPR”).
2. In this Policy: “User” means a natural person using the App or the Website; “Account” means a User's account in the App; “Message” means a text or voice message; “NiX” means a photo or video sent in the App; “Friend” means a User whose friend invitation has been accepted.
3. NiX is a ShipApp brand. The service is intended for persons aged 16 and over.

2. Controller

1. The controller of personal data is MT Hub Damian Motyliński, ul. ks. Józefa Poniatowskiego 27a lok. 2, 76-200 Słupsk, Poland, tax ID (NIP) 8393229228 (the “Controller”).
2. For all matters relating to the processing of personal data and the exercise of Users' rights, the Controller can be contacted at nix@shipapp.pl.
3. The Controller has not appointed a data protection officer.

3. Categories of personal data

1. The Controller processes the following categories of personal data:
a) Account data: email address, authentication data (a protected password or the Sign in with Apple identifier), Account identifier, confirmation of being aged 16 or over, and acceptance of the Terms and this Policy;
b) profile and relationship data: username, display name, profile bio, avatar, private-account setting, Friends, invitations and blocks;
c) communication content and metadata: Message content, voice recordings, NiXes, reactions, replies, forwards, save markers, earlier versions of edited Messages, NiX view time, and delivery and opened status;
d) conversation activity data: daily exchange counters (streaks and best friends), pinned and muted conversations, and attempts to capture a NiX by screenshot or screen recording;
e) safety data: reports, secured evidence and moderation decisions;
f) device data: notification token, device name and model, iOS and App version, language and date of last activity.
2. The Controller does not process location data, the device address book or advertising identifiers.
3. Providing data is voluntary but necessary to conclude and perform the service agreement; without it the App cannot be used.

4. Purposes and legal bases

1. Personal data is processed for the purposes of:
a) concluding and performing the service agreement, including maintaining the Account, connecting Friends and delivering Messages and NiXes — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR;
b) ensuring the security of the service, preventing abuse and prohibited content, handling reports, and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims — the Controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR);
c) complying with legal obligations, including providing information to competent authorities — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
2. The Controller does not process data for marketing purposes, does not display advertising, does not sell personal data and does not engage in tracking within the meaning of the App Store App Tracking Transparency rules.

5. Availability of Messages and NiXes

1. Text and voice Messages are deleted automatically 24 hours after they are sent.
2. Either participant in a conversation may mark a Message as saved. A saved Message is kept until either participant removes the save (after which it is deleted 24 hours later) or until the conversation is deleted.
3. The photo or video file of a NiX, together with its thumbnail, is deleted from the server approximately 10 minutes after the recipient first views it (the replay period). A NiX not viewed by its recipient is deleted 30 days after sending. Once the file is deleted, only the NiX's metadata remains in the conversation until the conversation is deleted.
4. The sender may edit a text Message within 15 minutes of sending (no more than five times) and undo sending within 5 minutes. Earlier versions of an edited Message are kept with it solely for the purpose of handling any report and are not made available to Users.
5. Forwarding a Message creates a new, separate Message whose availability period runs from the time it is sent.
6. Deleting a conversation deletes the Messages and NiXes of that pair of Users for both participants. Blocking a User hides the conversation and removes the save marker from that pair's Messages, which are then deleted 24 hours later.

6. Protection against capture

1. Screenshots and screen recordings are blocked by default while a NiX is displayed, and the sender is notified of any attempt.
2. The sender may allow selected Friends to save their NiXes. In that case the recipient may save the NiX to their device's photo library, and any further processing of that copy takes place outside the App and outside the Controller's control.
3. The protection described in paragraph 1 does not cover text and voice Messages and does not prevent content being captured with another device.

7. Automated content checks and moderation

1. Before delivery to the recipient, the text of Messages, photos and selected frames of videos are checked automatically using Microsoft Azure AI Content Safety (data centre in Sweden) and a blocked-words filter, solely to detect prohibited content (hate, violence, self-harm and sexual content). Content classified as prohibited is not delivered. The working copy used for the check is deleted once a decision is made, and no later than after 24 hours.
2. Voice recordings and the audio track of videos are not subject to automated checks.
3. A User may report a Message, NiX or profile of another User. In connection with a report, the Controller secures evidence (a copy of the photo, video or voice recording, or the Message text with its earlier versions) for 30 days.
4. Decisions to remove content and to restrict or suspend an Account are taken by the Controller's moderation team. Reports concerning child safety are handled as a priority and, to the extent required by law, passed to the competent authorities.

8. Private accounts and user search

1. A User can be found only by their exact username, or added with a QR code (valid for 5 minutes) or an invitation.
2. An Account marked as private does not appear in search results, and a friend connection with it can only be established using a code or invitation shared by its owner.
3. The App does not access the device address book and does not suggest contact with unknown Users.

9. Push notifications

1. Push notifications are sent only with the permission granted in iOS; the User may turn them off in the App or in the system settings.
2. A notification contains the sender's name and avatar and the type of event; it does not contain the Message content, photo or video. To present a notification as a message, iOS may store the sender's name and avatar on the device (for example for Siri suggestions and Focus).
3. A notification about an attempt to capture a NiX is sent regardless of the App's message-notification settings.

10. Device permissions

The App uses the camera, microphone and photo library only after permission is granted in iOS, in order to take photos, videos and voice recordings, scan QR codes, set an avatar and save to the photo library. Permission may be withdrawn at any time in the system settings, which makes the related feature unavailable.

11. Data stored only on the device

1. The “Memories” feature stores NiXes sent by the User only on their device, encrypted with AES-256 using a key held in the device Keychain. This data is not transmitted to the Controller and is not included in the data export. A device backup contains only encrypted files that cannot be read on another device. Memories are limited to 500 MB in total, and once the limit is exceeded the oldest items are deleted automatically.
2. The device also stores: an encrypted cache of received photos awaiting viewing; the number of new NiXes and upload progress shown in the widget and Live Activity (without names or content); and photos taken with the Lock Screen camera and files shared from other apps, until they are sent.
3. Signing out deletes Memories together with their key, the cache and the send queue from the device.

12. Recipients of data

1. Personal data is entrusted to processors only to the extent necessary to provide the service, under data processing agreements:
a) Supabase Inc. — authentication, database, file storage and server functions (data centre in Ireland);
b) Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd. (Azure AI Content Safety) — automated content checks (data centre in Sweden);
c) OVH SAS — a server preparing videos for checking (France);
d) Contabo GmbH — Website hosting (France);
e) Cloudflare, Inc. — Website delivery and protection;
f) 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo) — delivery of push notifications and App updates;
g) Apple Inc. / Apple Distribution International Ltd. — Sign in with Apple, notification delivery (APNs) and App distribution.
2. Data may be disclosed to public authorities only on the basis of applicable law.
3. Analytics and crash-reporting tools are disabled in the App. The Website does not use cookies or analytics tools.

13. Transfers to third countries

Users' personal data is stored within the European Economic Area. In connection with the use of Expo, Apple and Cloudflare, data necessary to deliver notifications, authenticate Users and operate the Website may be transferred to the United States — on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses, in accordance with Chapter V GDPR. A copy of the safeguards applied may be obtained from the Controller.

14. Retention periods

Personal data is kept for the following periods:
a) Account, profile, relationship, block, age-confirmation and document-acceptance data, pinned and muted conversations and streak counters — until the Account is deleted; device data — until the Account is deleted or after 180 days of inactivity;
b) Messages and NiXes — in accordance with section 5;
c) working copies used for automated content checks — until a decision is made, no longer than 24 hours;
d) evidence secured in connection with a report — 30 days; resolved reports — 365 days from resolution; moderation decisions concerning Accounts — for as long as necessary to keep the service secure;
e) notification delivery history — 30 days; the notification token is deleted on sign-out or Account deletion, and remains inactive when notifications are turned off in the App;
f) data export archives — 24 hours from preparation;
g) technical metadata concerning media uploads and deletion — 30 days; metadata of automated content checks — 30 days, or 90 days for content classified as prohibited;
h) data kept for longer where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims or where required by law — for the period resulting from those grounds.

15. Users' rights

1. Users have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on the Controller's legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).
2. Users can download a copy of their data themselves in the App: Profile → Privacy & security → Data export. Other requests should be sent to nix@shipapp.pl. The Controller responds without undue delay and in any event within one month of receiving the request.
3. Users have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland).

16. Account deletion

1. A User may delete their Account in the App: Profile → Account → Delete account, after confirming the username and re-authenticating. For an Account created with Sign in with Apple, the Controller also revokes the App's access to the User's Apple ID.
2. Deleting the Account deletes the Account, profile, relationships, Messages, NiXes, voice recordings, avatar and data exports. Files shared with other Users are deleted from the server within minutes. Only evidence secured in connection with reports (until the end of the period in section 14(d)) and report resolutions and moderation decisions without a link to the deleted Account are retained.
3. Signing out or deleting the App from a device does not delete the Account. A User who has lost access to the App may request deletion at nix@shipapp.pl from the email address associated with the Account.

17. Automated decision-making

The Controller does not take decisions concerning Users based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect them within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. The automated content checks described in section 7 concern only the delivery of a specific item of content; restriction or suspension of an Account follows a decision of the moderation team.

18. Minors

The service is intended for persons aged 16 and over. The date of birth is checked only on the device and is not stored; the Controller keeps only the confirmation of being aged 16 or over. If the Controller learns that a person under 16 is using the service, it deletes their Account.

19. Changes to this Policy

The Controller may amend this Policy, in particular following changes in the law or in the service. Users are informed in the App of material changes before they take effect. The current version of the Policy is available in the App and on the Website.
